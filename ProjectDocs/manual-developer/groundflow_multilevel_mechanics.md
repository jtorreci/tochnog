# groundflow multilevel mechanics — developer

## Scope

This note records the mechanics/pressure convention work of the ground11
closure (branch dev/d-ground8-mech): the pending item of the ground8 work
unit ("the mechanical coupling of MULTIPLE phreatic levels stays pending of
calibration: it requires aligning the dof convention between the `-pres`
loads (full head h) and the dynamic split") and its test vehicle

phreatic surface).

Reference binary for every A/B: the Professional 25-10-2023
(`tochnog_user_supplied`).

## Files and functions

- `data.cc` — `data()` reset-value application. The value records of a
  `control_reset_dof` now COMBINE: `control_reset_value_constant` + every
  active spatial distribution (`_linear`, `_power`, `_square_root`,
  `_exponent`, `_logarithmic*`, `_multi_linear`) + the
  `control_reset_value_dof` diagram are summed per node and the reset method
  (`-use/-add/-multiply`) applies to the combined value. Professional manual
  6.350: "The records control_reset_value_constant, control_reset_value_linear
  etc. can be arbitrarily combined so that complex dependency of the value of
  space coordinates is possible." The legacy GNU chain was an exclusive
  if/else-if: with a constant record at the index, the spatial (and dof)
  records were skipped.
- `group.cc` — `get_group_data()` dependency monitor resolution. The monitor
  of a `dependency_item` can be a dof label OR a `post_calcul_label`
  (Professional manual 6.406). The groundflow pressure post labels
  `-to_pres`/`-st_pres`/`-dy_pres` (generated by `post_calcul
  -groundflow_pressure -total_pressure` etc.) are now resolved: the element
  representative value = the average over the element nodes of
  `groundflow_phreatic_coord` totals/statics (total - static for dy_pres),
  evaluated on the current iterate dofs (`NODE_DOF` VERSION_NEW). The old
  code raised `db_error(DEPENDENCY_ITEM)` because the post labels are not in
  `dof_label`.
- `materi.cc` (guard zone of the phreatic-coord substitution) — the pore
  pressure acting on the skeleton (`new_pres`) becomes the
  `groundflow_phreatic_coord` TOTAL pressure when: (a) a single phreatic
  level / static-pressure-height region covers the integration point
  (previous behaviour, calibrated on ground15/16, ground13), OR (b) NO
  phreatic level / static-pressure-height machinery exists at all. Case (b)
  is measured on ground11_nonsaturated: the Professional total pressure with
  no reference records is `p_total = h - rho*g*z` (datum at z = 0, g and z
  negative below the datum, atmospheric clamp at 0) and the effective-stress
  equilibrium of the gravity-loaded column REQUIRES that hydrostatic part.
  With the raw pres dof the skeleton carries the full saturated weight while
  the reset stress covers only the buoyant part -> instantaneous collapse at
  the first step (veliy -0.39 at t=1 vs ~1e-15 in the Professional) and a
  vanishing hypoplasticity tangent at zero confining stress (singular solve).
  With the substitution the first step is in equilibrium exactly like the
  Professional. Multiple-level models keep the raw dof (measured guard of
  ground19: the head-prescribing `-pres` reservoir loads are full-head
  statics, adding the level static double counts rho*g*z_L and breaks the
  coupled system).

## Measured evidence (A/B vs the Professional binary, ground11 family)

- **Step-1 equilibrium** (post-fix): GNU veliy(t=1) = 0 (Professional
  1.15e-15) for both ground11_nonsaturated and ground11_phreatic_level; the
  early transient (t = 11..1e4 s) tracks the Professional within 5-15 %.
- **Draining phase** (water line falling to z=-50, ~1e6 s):
  - ground11_phreatic_level: GNU/Pro within 12-22 % through t=1e6
    (GNU -0.390 vs Pro -0.349 at t=1e6).
  - ground11_nonsaturated: within 5-10 % to t~1e5, then the GNU velocity
    decays to ~0 by t=9.7e5 while the Professional keeps settling (-0.35).
- **Re-wetting phase** (water line back to z=0, 1e6..2e6 s): the GNU
  unloads (elastic rebound; phreatic sibling final -0.267 vs Professional
  -0.356; nonsaturated sibling ~0.006 vs -0.366). The Professional keeps a
  near-constant settlement rate: the wetting collapse of the re-saturated
  (previously dry) zone.
- Targets: ground11_nonsaturated veliy(t=2e6) = +0.0056 vs -0.366 +- 0.01;
  ground11_phreatic_level = -0.267 vs -0.355 +- 0.02. Both run rc=1 on the
  numeric target (they were Bi-CG singular at step 1 / dependency crash
  before this work).
- reset1 of the corpus (temp = constant 1. + linear 1.23 x = 2.23 at x = 1)
  now PASSES rc=0 (was RUNFAIL): the combination semantics is pinned by the
  Professional test itself.
- hypo1 (constant-only reset) unchanged (-862.766, same value as HEAD).

## Pending (documented blocker with the design)

The remaining numeric gap of ground11 is NOT a pressure-convention issue: it
is the NON-SATURATED constitutive response of the skeleton under the
drying/wetting cycle (suction mechanics). The GNU hypoplasticity (wolffersdorff
via hypo.c) has no water-retention/wetting-collapse path: the re-saturated
zone unloads elastically instead of collapsing like the Professional. A full
non-saturated soil model (suction-dependent stiffness/strength, wetting
collapse on re-saturation, retention curve k(s)/theta(s) already present as
van Genuchten in groundda.cc) would be required to close the numeric targets;
that is a constitutive kernel work unit (hypo family, see the hypo1-13
calibration records), outside the pressure-convention scope of this batch.

## 2026-09-07 follow-up (dev/g11): wet/dry groundflow density switch

### Symptom re-measured (A/B 25-10-2023, ground11_phreatic_level)

At t = 1e6 (water line at z = -50) the GNU and the Professional carry the
SAME pore-pressure field but DIFFERENT effective stress in the drained zone:
with the reset buoyant profile as reference the GNU shows sigma'_yy(z=-50) =
-1003 (gradient 20.15/m above the line = rho_sat 2.015) while the
Professional shows -816 (gradient 16.3/m = rho_dry 1.630). The GNU used the
WET density in the drained zone: NODE_PHREATICLEVEL is only written by
groundflow_phreaticlevel_bounda, which the ground11 family does not use, so
get_materi_density never selected the dry density and the skeleton of the
drained zone carried an extra (rho_wet - rho_dry)*g*h of effective stress
(187 kPa at the level depth at t = 1e6).

### Fix (materi.cc, get_materi_density)

Manual Professional 6.641: "If the element is filled with groundwater the
density_wet will be used and otherwise the density_dry... In case total
pressures are calculated [post_calcul -groundflow_pressure -total_pressure]:
density_wet if the total pressure is smaller than 0, density_dry if larger
or equal to 0". The element decision now uses the average of the
groundflow_phreatic_coord totals over the element nodes on the current
iterate (NODE_DOF VERSION_NEW), exactly like the -to_pres dependency monitor
of group.cc; wet iff the average is < 0. Below the phreatic line / below a
static-pressure-height reference / below the implicit phreatic of a
no-reference model the total is negative (submerged); above it the total is
atmospheric-clamped to 0 -> dry. The old NODE_PHREATICLEVEL path is kept for
the non-groundflow case. After the fix the GNU sigma'_yy profile matches the
Professional within 0.3 % at every measured phase of both ground11 variants.

### Residual gap (still rc=1 on the numeric targets)

The density fix aligns the STRESS fields but does NOT close the settlement
targets (measured post-fix, full transient):
- ground11_phreatic_level: GNU veliy(t=2e6) = -0.431 vs -0.355 +- 0.02.
- ground11_nonsaturated: GNU = +0.00086 vs -0.366 +- 0.01.
At the SAME effective stress state (sigma'_yy within 0.3 %) the GNU column
has accumulated 6-8 % more local vertical strain and 11-21 % more settlement
than the Professional through the whole draining phase (ratio ~1.12 from
t = 1e3 to t = 1e6, independent of the permeability 1e-2..1e0, of the
autotimestep tolerance 1e-2..1e-6 and of the step count). Isolation probes
(single quad4 wolffersdorff oedometer, plane and axisymmetric, strain- and
step-count-independent, 10 vs 1000 steps) match the Professional to 0.7 %,
so the residual is NOT the raw wolffersdorff kernel response on standard
paths. It is a path-level difference of the coupled multi-element column
(the lateral stress response of the column is ~frozen, Delta-sigma_h ~ 0 for
Delta-sigma_v = -311 kPa, in BOTH binaries - an artifact of the axisymmetric
stress-dof formulation shared by the reference - while the single-element
oedometer gives the constitutive K0 increment 0.38; on that frozen-lateral
path the hypo accumulation differs by ~6-8 %), which needs a dedicated
constitutive/coupling work unit (hypo family) to close.

### Side effect measured

ground4 of the corpus (phreatic level at -50 with rho_wet/rho_dry 2.0/1.5)
goes rc=1 -> rc=0 with the wet/dry density selection (its target checks the
buoyancy of the drained zone). No regression on ground6/8/14/15/16/19,
undrained1/2, large2/3, reset1, hypo1/2/4/7/8/9 and the internal suite
16/16.
