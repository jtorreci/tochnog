# control_mesh_merge_geometry / control_mesh_merge_geometry_not

## Description

Geometrical scoping of the node merging of `control_mesh_merge` (manual
Professional 6.214/6.215). Both records share the control index of the
`control_mesh_merge` record they qualify:

```
control_mesh_merge              60 -yes
control_mesh_merge_geometry     60 -geometry_line 1        (merge only on the geometry)
control_mesh_merge_geometry_not 60 -geometry_line 2 -geometry_point 5   (do NOT merge on these)
```

- `control_mesh_merge_geometry <index> <entity> <n> ...`: when present,
  the merging of `control_mesh_merge` (same index) applies ONLY to the
  nodes lying on the listed geometries.
- `control_mesh_merge_geometry_not <index> <entity> <n> ...`: the
  merging does NOT apply to the nodes lying on the listed geometries.
  Pairs of a geometry keyword and its index may be repeated in one
  record (the `-wall` example below expands from a `start_define`).

Typical use: merge two meshes everywhere EXCEPT along an interface line
that must stay split (double nodes) so that the two sides can be tied
with `mpc_element_group` (cut-off walls, sheet piles, ...).

The older GNU spelling `control_mesh_merge_not` is accepted as an alias
of `control_mesh_merge_geometry_not`, and the Professional
`control_mesh_merge_eps_coord` as an alias of
`control_mesh_merge_epscoord` (both resolved in `db_number`).

## Example

```
start_define wall geometry_line 2 end_define

control_mesh_merge              60 -yes
control_mesh_merge_macro_generate 60 40 50
control_mesh_merge_geometry_not 60 -wall
```

Nodes of the meshes generated by macros 40 and 50 merge, except the
nodes on the wall line `2`, which keep double nodes (see
[mpc_element_group](mpc_element_group.md) for tying them afterwards).
