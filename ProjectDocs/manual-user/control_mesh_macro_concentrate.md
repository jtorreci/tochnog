# control_mesh_macro_concentrate

Mesh fineness concentration of the `-rectangle` mesh macros (manual
Professional 6.207): two factors per direction (at the beginning and at the
end of the direction; a smaller factor = smaller elements). Example used by
the corpus heat_exchanger models: `control_mesh_macro_concentrate 11 1 7 1 1`.
